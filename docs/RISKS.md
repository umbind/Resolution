# Risk Assessment & Mitigation Plan: Resolution

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-01** | **Defamatory Naming of Teachers/Staff**<br>Users might vent frustrations by publicly naming specific teachers, leading to slander allegations or hostility. | **Critical** | High | 1. Mandatory upfront guidelines modal.<br>2. Automated client-side regex + AI filter blocking honorifics + personal names.<br>3. Instant 1-tap UGC report and block buttons.<br>4. Redirect administrative complaints to official Jansunwai 1076. |
| **R-02** | **Child Data & Privacy Violation (DPDP Act 2023)**<br>Exposing names, photos, or phone numbers of minors (<18 years), risking statutory fines up to ₹250 crore. | **Critical** | Medium | 1. Strict zero-exposure policy: phone numbers and emails are never public.<br>2. Minor flag with mandatory parental consent confirmation.<br>3. Ban face photos of minors in public feeds.<br>4. No tracking, profiling, or targeted ads of children. |
| **R-03** | **Google Play UGC Policy Rejection**<br>Failure to provide native in-app report/block buttons and timely moderation queue. | **High** | Medium | 1. Implement report button on every issue and solution card.<br>2. Implement user blocking in profile and cards.<br>3. Terms of service acceptance required before first post.<br>4. 24-hour moderation queue via Firebase. |
| **R-04** | **Low-End Android Device Lag & OOM**<br>Target users on 2GB RAM Android Go devices experiencing crashes or UI stutter. | **High** | High | 1. Pinned Compose BOM with R8 minification.<br>2. Local image compression (<500KB) before upload; Coil memory cache capped at 15%.<br>3. Zero heavy third-party animation libraries (Lottie/Rive avoided).<br>4. Cold start benchmark target < 1.8 seconds. |
| **R-05** | **Political Hijacking of Education Grievances**<br>Electoral parties or activists using the platform for partisan rallies and mudslinging. | **Medium** | High | 1. Clear charter: strictly non-political, education-only.<br>2. Keywords filter for political party names and electoral campaigns.<br>3. Community flags prioritize political posts for immediate review. |
| **R-06** | **AI Hallucinations & Fabricated Schemes**<br>Gemini inventing non-existent government funding schemes or legal guarantees. | **Medium** | Medium | 1. Grounded prompts with strict negative constraints ("Never invent government schemes").<br>2. Prominent "AI द्वारा संकलित (AI-Generated)" badge with user editability.<br>3. Disclaimer advising community verification. |
| **R-07** | **Intermittent Rural Connectivity (2G/3G)**<br>Network drops causing lost drafts or blank screens. | **High** | High | 1. Room DB offline-first architecture; all feeds cached locally.<br>2. Issue and solution drafts auto-saved every 3 seconds to local storage.<br>3. Background sync with WorkManager when network restores. |
