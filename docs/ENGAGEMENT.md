# Healthy Engagement & Social Proof Plan: Resolution

## 1. Principles of Ethical Engagement (Zero Dark Patterns)
In alignment with our user base (parents, rural educators, and students under 18), Resolution explicitly rejects toxic retention mechanics:
* **No Streak Pressure:** Users are never punished, guilt-tripped, or notified about "losing a streak".
* **No Artificial Urgency:** No countdown timers or false scarcity ("Act now or miss out!").
* **No Public Minor Rankings:** Students are never ranked against each other; achievements are strictly skill- and impact-based.
* **Recognition Over Vanity:** Badges represent verified community service (e.g. "5 स्कूलों में किताबें पहुंचाईं"), not sheer post volume.

## 3. Notification Policy & Quiet Hours
To respect user attention and avoid battery drain on low-end devices:
1. **Hard Daily Cap:** Maximum 3 push notifications per user per day.
2. **Strict Quiet Hours:** Zero push notifications between **9:00 PM and 8:00 AM IST**.
3. **Trigger Events Only:**
   - A solution is added to your posted issue.
   - A resolution is marked as "Tried and Worked".
   - An issue in your block reaches "Best Practical Plan" status.
   - Weekly Digest (Single optional summary every Sunday morning).
4. **1-Tap Mute:** Every notification category can be individually toggled off in settings.

## 4. Key Engagement & Impact Metrics (Privacy-Respecting)

Analytics are captured locally and aggregated with explicit user consent, without storing personal identifiers or tracking device IDs:

| Metric | Target (Post-Launch) | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding Completion Rate** | > 85% | Users completing splash -> home feed |
| **Time to First Post** | < 60 seconds | From tapping "Post" to submitting issue |
| **Solution-to-Issue Ratio** | > 2.5 solutions per issue | Total approved solutions / Total issues |
| **"Tried & Worked" Rate** | > 20% of issues | Issues transitioning to `resolved` status |
| **WhatsApp Share Rate** | > 35% of viewed plans | Taps on WhatsApp share card |
| **Notification Opt-out Rate** | < 8% | Users completely muting notifications |
