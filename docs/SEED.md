# Product Seed Brief: पढ़ाई की चौपाल (Padhai Ki Chaupal)

**App name:** पढ़ाई की चौपाल  
**Tagline (Hindi):** "सवाल पूछो, आइडिया बाँटो"  
**Tagline (English):** "Ask questions, share learning ideas"  

Android community platform where teachers, parents, students, and education volunteers share practical ideas on **HOW TO LEARN AND STUDY**: study methods, creative learning activities, teaching tricks, exam preparation, and answers to everyday learning challenges. 

First focus: Uttar Pradesh, India. Hindi is the primary language, English second. Designed for low-end phones and intermittent connectivity. The architecture supports other domains (farming, health) and states later without rebuilding.

## Key Content & Post Model:
1. **Two Core Post Types:**
   - **सवाल या चुनौती (Question or challenge):** e.g., "कक्षा 5 के बच्चे को भिन्न (fractions) खेल-खेल में कैसे सिखाएं?", "पहाड़े याद करने में कठिनाई हो रही है।"
   - **आइडिया या गतिविधि (Idea or activity):** e.g., "5 मिनट का गणित का खेल: कंकड़ों से जोड़-घटाना", "रोटी के टुकड़ों से भिन्न समझाना।"
2. **9 Core Categories:**
   - पढ़ाई के तरीके व तकनीक (Study methods)
   - गणित और विज्ञान गतिविधियाँ (Maths and science activities)
   - पढ़ना और लिखना (Reading and writing)
   - अंग्रेजी व भाषा सीखें (English and language learning)
   - परीक्षा की तैयारी व तनाव मुक्ति (Exam preparation and stress)
   - कम खर्च की शिक्षण सामग्री / TLM (Low-cost activities)
   - कक्षा में पढ़ाने के तरीके (Classroom teaching methods)
   - करियर और हुनर (Careers and skills)
   - घर पर बच्चों की पढ़ाई में मदद (Support for parents at home)
3. **Structured "Activity Card" (गतिविधि कार्ड):**
   - Class or age group (कक्षा या आयु वर्ग: कक्षा 1-3, कक्षा 4-5, आदि)
   - Time needed (समय: 5-10 मिनट, 15-20 मिनट)
   - Materials needed (कम खर्च/घरेलू सामग्री: माचिस की तीलियां, कंकड़, बोतल के ढक्कन)
   - Step-by-step method (चरणबद्ध तरीका)
   - Expected learning outcome (सीखने का प्रतिफल / क्या सीखेगा बच्चा)
   - "Tried it and it worked" count (प्रमाणित: उपाय काम कर गया)
   - Optional adaptation notes (अन्य कक्षाओं के लिए सुझाव)
4. **Replies as Solutions:**
   - Responses to questions are structured ideas or activities.
   - Community voting, "Tried it, it worked" verification tags, expert educator badges, and WhatsApp sharing.
5. **AI Features (via Server-side Gemini):**
   - **Activity Coach:** Critiques a draft idea for clarity, safety, and age-appropriateness.
   - **Duplicate Question Detection & Clustering:** Merges similar student/teacher queries.
   - **Best Study Plan:** Synthesizes top ideas into a simple, step-by-step weekly routine for a question.
   - Guardrails: Strictly NO medical/psychological advice, NO fabricated studies or schemes, disclaimers when unsure.
6. **Exclusions & Moderation:**
   - School infrastructure complaints (broken toilets, handpumps, teacher shortages) are strictly excluded and flagged.
   - Political content and named accusations against teachers, schools, or students are blocked and flagged.
